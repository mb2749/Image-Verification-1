# Image-Verification-1
Determining the original date of a news photograph using open source intelligence
An article published today (Wednesday 19th August, 2026) in the Irish Times about Finland preparing for potential Russian hostility included a photograph of Finnish border patrol in Imatra, Finland, presented in a current context. 
When I reverse-image searched this photograph I found it featured in an article in YLE, an online Finnish news site, from 2023.
After confirming that the image wasn't recent, I found the original image on Getty Images, from the 26th October, 2023, demonstrating that the image predates the news article by over two years.
